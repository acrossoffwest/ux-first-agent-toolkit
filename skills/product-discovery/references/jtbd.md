# Jobs To Be Done — interview prompts and traps

## Prompt set

- "Walk me through the last time you <did the task>. What triggered it?"
- "What did you try before? Why did you stop using it?"
- "What would make you switch away from your current way?"
- "If this product disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead?"
- "What does 'done' look like for you here?"

## Format

> When <situation/trigger>, I want <motivation>, so I can <expected outcome>.

The situation is the most important part — it scopes the design. "I want a
dashboard" is a solution, not a job. Dig until you reach the outcome.

## Traps

- **Feature-shaped answers.** Users describe solutions ("add a calendar").
  Ask what the calendar would let them accomplish; record the job, not the widget.
- **Imagined users.** A persona nobody interviewed is `assumed`, never `verified`.
- **Averaging.** Two different jobs merged into one vague persona produce a
  product for nobody. Keep jobs separate even when one person holds both.
- **Frequency blindness.** A dramatic but rare job can displace the boring
  daily one. Note frequency next to each job.
